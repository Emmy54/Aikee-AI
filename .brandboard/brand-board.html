II

Colour

Ledger black and antique bronze on warm paper. Six colours with a job each; bronze splits into a fill and a darker text-safe sibling so it never fails contrast.

Buttons: bronze fill with ledger-black label (6.08:1). Never white text on bronze (3.25:1 — fails). On paper, bronze is only ever a fill or a rule; when bronze must be read on light, switch to Bronze Text #8A6408.

III

Two families. Fraunces, an optical serif, gives the editorial, hand-set voice; Inter keeps the interface plain and legible. A major-third scale (1.25) throughout.

V

Three don’ts

The rules that keep the identity dignified and legible. Restraint is the point.

Overlooked  →  Overlooked
Don’t

Set bronze #B8860B as small text on light — it fails at 2.94:1. Use Bronze Text #8A6408 for anything read on paper.

Build  →  Build
Don’t

Put white text on a bronze button (3.25:1). Bronze buttons take a ledger-black label — 6.08:1, and it looks like struck metal.

💡⚙️🧠
Don’t

Reach for AI/tech clichés — circuit boards, neural nodes, lightbulbs — or decorative “pattern” shorthand. The serif and the signature line carry the meaning.
